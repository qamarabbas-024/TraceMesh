# Decision Log

## Purpose

Preserve important reasoning so future contributors and AI agents do not accidentally undo deliberate decisions.

## Record When

Record decisions that materially affect product direction, architecture, technology, security, UX, dependencies, data, scope, business model, or operations.

## Format

For each important decision record:

- Decision title
- Date
- Context/problem
- Options considered
- Evidence
- Decision
- Why it was chosen
- Trade-offs
- Rejected alternatives
- Consequences
- Revisit condition, if any

## Rule

Do not log every trivial implementation choice. Record durable decisions that future work may reasonably question.
